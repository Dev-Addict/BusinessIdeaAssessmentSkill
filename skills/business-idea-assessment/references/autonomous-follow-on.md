# Autonomous follow-on work

Read this after the initial assessment or when continuing an existing package.
The purpose is to close desk-solvable gaps and make human validation faster—not
to simulate evidence that requires the outside world.

## Select the work

Build a candidate backlog from unresolved assumptions, risks, and route gates.
Rank each task on:

- decision value: could it change advance/revise/stop or route selection?
- evidence gap: is the current claim low confidence or decision-critical?
- autonomy: can it be completed with research, calculation, drafting, or local
  files?
- dependency: does it unblock later interviews, quotes, trials, or approvals?
- reversibility and cost: prefer reversible work with no external spend.

Execute the highest-value safe tasks. Do not merely recommend them.

## Common autonomous deliverables

Choose only those relevant to the idea:

- draft outreach pack and contact/activity tracker; do not send;
- 13-week cash-control model with commitments and stop triggers;
- compliance, label, claims, privacy, insurance, or permit preflight;
- supplier/partner/contractor RFQ and apples-to-apples quote comparison;
- expanded partner, manufacturer, distributor, platform, or facility search;
- product, packaging, data, service-level, or technical specification;
- founder operating system, evidence register, dashboard, decision log, and
  document register;
- customer interview, paid test, cohort, repeat/retention, or retail-pilot kit;
- funding action calendar with pre-approval cautions;
- 30/60/90-day plan tied to evidence gates and cash;
- refreshed sensitivities and downside/stress cases.

Store work in `next_steps/`, add a nested README, and link every file from both
the nested and root README.

## Do not fabricate external progress

The following remain human/external tasks until actually completed:

- regulator or professional determinations;
- supplier quotes and negotiated terms;
- interviews, surveys, commitments, purchase orders, and signed contracts;
- laboratory, engineering, security, safety, or product tests;
- approvals, funding awards, licences, certifications, and insurance coverage;
- paid conversion, retention, repeat purchase, store velocity, or production
  yield.

Create the request, protocol, comparison table, or record template that will
capture the evidence. Mark every unsent/unrun artifact clearly.

## Stop conditions

Stop autonomous expansion and ask the user when:

- two or more strategic routes remain equally plausible and the choice changes
  the deliverable substantially;
- the next step would create an account, contact a person, incur cost, accept
  terms, submit data, publish, or change external state;
- required proprietary inputs are unavailable and assumptions would dominate
  the result; or
- the user must choose a risk tolerance, founder-income target, capital limit,
  or ethical boundary that cannot responsibly be inferred.
